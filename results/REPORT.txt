Two pre-committed books, not a factor zoo. A CI that covers zero means the mean IC is not distinguishable from noise at 95%, even if a single Sharpe looks large. Industry portfolios have less idiosyncratic noise than stocks, so ICs here run higher than a stock-level study and should not be pasted onto a stock-trading resume line.
